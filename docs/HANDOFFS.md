## Receiving receipt

Record producer artifact and immutable revision; source and evidence conditions; exact fields and intended uses; schema/taxonomy versions; consumer issue and revision; reviewer identity and actual decision URL/date; limitations; compatibility/migration assumptions; correction and rollback behavior; unresolved follow-ups. Until those exist, use `pending`, never approved or delivered.

## Website research requirements

The handoff must support discoverable category pages, multi-axis filters, stable canonical URLs and alias redirects, accessible comparison tables, visible source dates and evidence labels, exact plan/region pricing conditions, explicit unknown/withheld fields, retirement history and a correction channel. Localization, search relevance, exports/API terms and SEO are consumer requirements for review; frontend implementation remains in the website repository.
