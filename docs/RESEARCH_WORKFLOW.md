# Applications research workflow

## 1. Scope a real user decision

Read the assigned issue, taxonomy inclusion/exclusion, overlap ownership and canonical source policies. Separate application, product family, feature, edition/plan, release, provider/operator and implementation component. A suite can have multiple application experiences; a branded feature is not automatically a standalone app. Document identity reasoning rather than maximizing counts.

## 2. Discover and record candidates

Use a reproducible, bounded discovery method across official product pages, documentation, repositories, supported app stores and specialist sources. A directory or search result is a discovery lead. Inspect the product's own current material before making a claim. Capture query/source/date/language/region, deduplication decisions, inclusion/exclusion reasons and saturation limitations. Do not start a crawler or paid service merely because a source exists.

Keep incomplete information in `research/notes/` and the category decision record. Known candidates, deduplicated products, checked claims, reviewed records, independently reproduced claims and publishable records have separate counters.

## 3. Check claims and conditions

Record public primary URL, publisher, access date, exact product and release/plan/platform/region/language conditions. Distinguish primary documentation, vendor claim, independent measurement and estimate. Feature matrix states are Exercised, Documented, Unsupported and Unknown. A failed check is not a negative finding. A documented capability remains unverified until the required independent evidence exists.

Research privacy, data use, retention, training opt-out, deployment, account controls, accessibility, integrations, limits, pricing and lifecycle as separate scoped claims. Do not turn an unaudited security badge, generic regulatory statement, supported language list or marketing case study into a legal, usability or performance guarantee.

## 4. Normalize without inventing facts

Use the local contract for complete structured research records; notes can remain incomplete. Put application metadata in `data/applications/` and plan-conditioned pricing in `data/pricing/`. Do not fill missing rates with zero, missing booleans with false or unconfirmed models with guessed names. Store aliases and lifecycle relationships with evidence and historical scope.

## 5. Review evidence and intended uses

Obtain actual independent review of the exact facts and change. Record source/field/intended-use rights decisions separately. Unknown or restricted permission keeps affected fields out of public projections. An eligible factual field does not automatically make screenshots, vendor descriptions or prices eligible. Keep conflicting sources and correction history visible.

## 6. Deliver a bounded consumer handoff

Provide a versioned contract, eligible projection, provenance, withheld-field reasons, freshness/correction semantics and receiving tests to the catalog-pipeline and website owners. Their implementations remain in their repositories. Source checked, reviewed, eligible, released and visible on the website are separately demonstrated outcomes.

## 7. Maintain and close carefully

Report changes, deprecations, aliases, changed terms, withdrawn rights and broken sources. Recheck current-state facts using an explicit risk-based policy; there is no universal freshness period inherited from #41. Keep retirement history rather than deleting identities. Close a package only when its decision record, acceptance and required handoffs are complete. Update coverage from actual records, never from issue creation counts.

## Validation boundaries

Run the commands in README. Offline validation checks identity, dates, required evidence structures, reference integrity, planning hierarchy and coverage semantics. Actual source access, rights interpretation, non-author approval, independent reproduction and receiving acceptance still require review. A passing empty directory explicitly reports zero real records.

Public GitHub commits are themselves publication and can enable bulk download. Keeping material out of a future website export does not authorize placing restricted or unapproved copied material in research notes, examples or other public repository files. Use original summaries and links under the research policy; raw source material requires its own permitted-use basis.
