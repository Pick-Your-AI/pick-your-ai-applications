# Meeting, speech and communication assistance

## User decision

Applications for capturing, understanding and coordinating human conversations and meetings.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- ASR and diarization models remain T1 components.
- Speaker attribution and consent handling require their own evidence; recording availability proves neither.

## Deliverables

- `research/categories/meetings-communication.md`
- `research/candidates/meetings-communication.json`
- `coverage/ledger.json`

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted meeting, speech and communication assistance workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for live versus recorded workflows, speaker/language and timestamp behavior, consent and recording controls?
- What user choices are changed by meeting integrations and permissions, retention, search and action export, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?

## Evidence and comparison rules

Use dated primary sources and the application contract. Bind claims to the exact product, release/channel, plan, platform, region and other applicable conditions. Preserve Unknown; source checked is not independently verified. Review, source-use eligibility and consumer delivery are separate. Record vendor statements, independent measurements and estimates distinctly.
