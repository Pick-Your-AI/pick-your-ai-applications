# Writing, editing and editorial publishing

## User decision

Tools for drafting, rewriting, proofreading, authoring and editorial review of written content.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- Generic model text-generation capability is not a product workflow.
- Authorship or detection accuracy cannot be inferred from a product name or marketing claim.

## Deliverables

- `research/categories/writing-editorial.md`
- `research/candidates/writing-editorial.json`
- `coverage/ledger.json`

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted writing, editing and editorial publishing workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for document and language workflow coverage, citation and factual-review workflow, editorial controls and collaboration?
- What user choices are changed by export formats and portability, content, output and training-use terms, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?

## Evidence and comparison rules

Use dated primary sources and the application contract. Bind claims to the exact product, release/channel, plan, platform, region and other applicable conditions. Preserve Unknown; source checked is not independently verified. Review, source-use eligibility and consumer delivery are separate. Record vendor statements, independent measurements and estimates distinctly.
