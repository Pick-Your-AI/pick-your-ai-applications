# Project, product and portfolio management

## User decision

Applications helping teams define, plan, coordinate and evaluate projects and products.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- A general summarizer is not automatically project-management software.
- Delivery forecasts and prioritization scores are contextual estimates unless measured.

## Deliverables

- `research/categories/project-product-management.md`
- `research/candidates/project-product-management.json`
- `coverage/ledger.json`

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted project, product and portfolio management workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for work tracking and integration scope, decision and source traceability, planning assumptions and uncertainty?
- What user choices are changed by team permissions and collaboration, export, history and change audit, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?

## Evidence and comparison rules

Use dated primary sources and the application contract. Bind claims to the exact product, release/channel, plan, platform, region and other applicable conditions. Preserve Unknown; source checked is not independently verified. Review, source-use eligibility and consumer delivery are separate. Record vendor statements, independent measurements and estimates distinctly.

Category comparison fields:

- Work tracking and integration scope
- Decision and source traceability
- Planning assumptions and uncertainty
- Team permissions and collaboration
- Export, history and change audit
