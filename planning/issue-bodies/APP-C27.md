# People operations, recruitment and workforce

## User decision

Applications for recruitment, HR services, workforce planning and employee development.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- Applicant datasets and predictive models alone remain data/components.
- Fairness, employment-law compliance and candidate suitability require scoped evidence.

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted people operations, recruitment and workforce workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for decision and human-review boundaries, candidate/employee data provenance, accessibility and bias evaluation scope?
- What user choices are changed by hris and workflow integrations, jurisdiction, retention and audit controls, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?

Preserve Unknown; source checked is not independently verified. Review, source-use eligibility and consumer delivery are separate. Record vendor statements, independent measurements and estimates distinctly.
