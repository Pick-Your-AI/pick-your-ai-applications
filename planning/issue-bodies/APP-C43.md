# Geospatial, earth observation and surveying

## User decision

Applications analyzing or authoring location-based, satellite, aerial and spatial information.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- Imagery datasets, remote-sensing models and sensor platforms remain separate entities.
- Map output does not establish survey accuracy or lawful surveillance use.

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted geospatial, earth observation and surveying workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for spatial/temporal resolution and coverage, coordinate and reference-system behavior, source and licensing provenance?
- What user choices are changed by accuracy, uncertainty and review, access, export and use restrictions, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?
