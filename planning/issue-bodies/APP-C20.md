# Browser, computer and device agents

## User decision

End-user applications that observe and act through browser, desktop or mobile interfaces.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- Automation libraries and browser-control protocols remain components.
- UI control availability is not proof of task reliability, authority or safe action execution.

## Deliverables

- `research/categories/computer-browser-agents.md`
- `research/candidates/computer-browser-agents.json`
- `coverage/ledger.json`

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted browser, computer and device agents workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for supported surfaces and action scope, approval and sensitive-action boundaries, credential and session handling?
- What user choices are changed by recovery, audit trail and reversibility, task reliability evidence and limitations, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?
