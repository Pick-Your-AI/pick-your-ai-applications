# Healthcare delivery and clinical administration

## User decision

Products supporting care workflows, clinical documentation, medical information and health-system administration.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- Models, medical datasets and generic research papers remain with their owners.
- Category membership or marketing language is not regulatory clearance, clinical validation or medical advice.

## Deliverables

- `research/categories/healthcare.md`
- `research/candidates/healthcare.json`
- `coverage/ledger.json`

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted healthcare delivery and clinical administration workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for intended use and patient population, jurisdiction and actual clearance scope, clinical evaluation and human oversight?
- What user choices are changed by health-data handling and integration, version, deployment and operational restrictions, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?

## Evidence and comparison rules

Use dated primary sources and the application contract. Bind claims to the exact product, release/channel, plan, platform, region and other applicable conditions. Preserve Unknown; source checked is not independently verified. Review, source-use eligibility and consumer delivery are separate. Record vendor statements, independent measurements and estimates distinctly.
