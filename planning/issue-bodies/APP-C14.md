# Workflow automation and enterprise agent applications

## User decision

Configurable products that coordinate repeatable business work and actions across software systems.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- Agent frameworks, MCP servers and workflow execution libraries remain T4 components.
- An integration listing does not establish an authorized end-to-end business action.

## Deliverables

- `research/categories/workflow-automation.md`
- `research/candidates/workflow-automation.json`
- `coverage/ledger.json`

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted workflow automation and enterprise agent applications workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for supported triggers/actions and limits, human approval and permission controls, state, retries, idempotency and recovery?
- What user choices are changed by auditability and action reversibility, connector terms and credential boundaries, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?

## Evidence and comparison rules

Use dated primary sources and the application contract. Bind claims to the exact product, release/channel, plan, platform, region and other applicable conditions. Preserve Unknown; source checked is not independently verified. Review, source-use eligibility and consumer delivery are separate. Record vendor statements, independent measurements and estimates distinctly.

- Human approval and permission controls
