# Enterprise search and knowledge work

## User decision

Applications that search, synthesize, organize or answer from organizational knowledge with user-facing discovery and collaboration.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- Vector databases, embedding models, RAG pipelines and storage components remain T3.
- Connector presence does not prove authorized retrieval or ACL enforcement.

## Deliverables

- `research/categories/enterprise-knowledge.md`
- `research/candidates/enterprise-knowledge.json`
- `coverage/ledger.json`

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted enterprise search and knowledge work workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for indexed source families and connector scope, permission and identity boundaries, citation and answer provenance?
- What user choices are changed by index freshness and deletion handling, tenant, deployment and residency options, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?

## Evidence and comparison rules

Use dated primary sources and the application contract. Bind claims to the exact product, release/channel, plan, platform, region and other applicable conditions. Preserve Unknown; source checked is not independently verified. Review, source-use eligibility and consumer delivery are separate. Record vendor statements, independent measurements and estimates distinctly.
