# Scientific research, literature and discovery

## User decision

Applications for literature discovery, evidence synthesis and computational scientific work across disciplines.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- Papers, datasets and model releases are linked entities, not duplicate application entries.
- Generated citations and experimental suggestions require validation.

## Deliverables

- `research/categories/science-research.md`
- `research/candidates/science-research.json`
- `coverage/ledger.json`

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted scientific research, literature and discovery workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for corpus and discipline coverage, citation and revision traceability, reproducibility and artifact export?
- What user choices are changed by research data and source-use terms, human review and methodological limitations, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?

## Evidence and comparison rules

Use dated primary sources and the application contract. Bind claims to the exact product, release/channel, plan, platform, region and other applicable conditions. Preserve Unknown; source checked is not independently verified. Review, source-use eligibility and consumer delivery are separate. Record vendor statements, independent measurements and estimates distinctly.

Category comparison fields:

- Corpus and discipline coverage
- Citation and revision traceability
- Reproducibility and artifact export
- Research data and source-use terms
- Human review and methodological limitations
