# Manufacturing, engineering and industrial work

## User decision

Applications assisting industrial design, production, maintenance and operational decision-making.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- Accelerators, sensors, robots and predictive models remain separate component assets.
- Simulation or anomaly detection does not establish physical safety or production yield.

## Deliverables

- `research/categories/manufacturing-engineering.md`
- `research/candidates/manufacturing-engineering.json`
- `coverage/ledger.json`

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted manufacturing, engineering and industrial work workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for asset/process and environment scope, measurement and validation basis, operator review and control boundaries?
- What user choices are changed by industrial system integrations, deployment, continuity and data controls, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?

## Evidence and comparison rules

Use dated primary sources and the application contract. Bind claims to the exact product, release/channel, plan, platform, region and other applicable conditions. Preserve Unknown; source checked is not independently verified. Review, source-use eligibility and consumer delivery are separate. Record vendor statements, independent measurements and estimates distinctly.
