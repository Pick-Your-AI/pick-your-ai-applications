# Robotics, autonomy and physical-agent software

## User decision

Identifiable software applications for configuring, supervising or operating embodied autonomous systems.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- Robot hardware, control models, research policies and inference runtimes remain separate linked entities.
- Simulation or product availability does not establish real-world safety, certification or autonomous authority.

- `research/categories/robotics-autonomy.md`

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted robotics, autonomy and physical-agent software workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for embodiment and operational design domain, operator oversight and emergency controls, simulation versus deployed evidence?
- What user choices are changed by safety, certification and geographic scope, version, integration and data boundaries, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?
