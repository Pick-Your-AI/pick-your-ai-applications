# Document intelligence and extraction

## User decision

Applications for reading, structuring, analyzing and transforming documents, forms and records.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- Bare OCR models, parsers and extraction APIs without a defined end-user workflow remain component candidates.
- A document upload demonstration does not establish production accuracy or sensitive-data eligibility.

## Deliverables

- `research/categories/document-intelligence.md`
- `research/candidates/document-intelligence.json`
- `coverage/ledger.json`

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted document intelligence and extraction workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for file types, page and size limits, extraction schema and validation workflow, human correction and review?
- What user choices are changed by batch and integration interfaces, retention, redaction and permission behavior, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?

## Evidence and comparison rules

Use dated primary sources and the application contract. Bind claims to the exact product, release/channel, plan, platform, region and other applicable conditions. Preserve Unknown; source checked is not independently verified. Review, source-use eligibility and consumer delivery are separate. Record vendor statements, independent measurements and estimates distinctly.
