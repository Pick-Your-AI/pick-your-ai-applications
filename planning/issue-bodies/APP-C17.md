# Audio, voice and music creation

## User decision

Applications for generating, editing, producing and managing speech, music and sound.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- Underlying TTS, music and audio models remain T1.
- Voice availability does not imply consent, commercial rights or impersonation permission.

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted audio, voice and music creation workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for creation and editing controls, supported audio formats and delivery, consent and voice-use controls?
- What user choices are changed by commercial/output licensing conditions, metering, credits and media limits, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?
