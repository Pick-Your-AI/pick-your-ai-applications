# Personal wellness, fitness and sports

## User decision

Consumer and professional applications for nonclinical wellbeing, fitness, coaching and sports analysis.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- Clinical care tools belong APP-C33; devices and sensors are separate assets.
- Wellness or sports benefit claims do not establish clinical effectiveness or safe personalization.

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted personal wellness, fitness and sports workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for user/activity and intended-use scope, evidence and limits of recommendations, wearable and health-data integrations?
- What user choices are changed by human support and escalation, privacy, age and commercial terms, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?
