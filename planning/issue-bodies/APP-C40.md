# Architecture, construction and real estate

## User decision

Applications for the design, delivery, operation and transaction of buildings and property.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- Design models, geospatial datasets and building hardware remain separate entities.
- A design or estimate does not establish code compliance, valuation accuracy or construction safety.

## Deliverables

- `research/categories/built-environment.md`
- `research/candidates/built-environment.json`
- `coverage/ledger.json`

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted architecture, construction and real estate workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for project phase and professional role, drawing/bim/data integrations, dimensional and cost evidence?
- What user choices are changed by jurisdiction and review requirements, property and client data handling, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?

## Evidence and comparison rules

Use dated primary sources and the application contract. Bind claims to the exact product, release/channel, plan, platform, region and other applicable conditions. Preserve Unknown; source checked is not independently verified. Review, source-use eligibility and consumer delivery are separate. Record vendor statements, independent measurements and estimates distinctly.
