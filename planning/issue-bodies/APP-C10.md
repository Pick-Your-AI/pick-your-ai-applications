# Application, website and low-code builders

## User decision

Products that create or materially modify usable software applications, websites and interfaces from user instructions.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- Hosting services, language models and scaffolding libraries alone are components.
- Generated preview, exportable project and production deployment are separate states.

## Deliverables

- `research/categories/application-builders.md`
- `research/candidates/application-builders.json`
- `coverage/ledger.json`

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted application, website and low-code builders workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for generated artifact ownership and export, backend, data and authentication scope, deployment and hosting options?
- What user choices are changed by editing, collaboration and versioning, usage limits, credits and recurring charges, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?

## Evidence and comparison rules

Use dated primary sources and the application contract. Bind claims to the exact product, release/channel, plan, platform, region and other applicable conditions. Preserve Unknown; source checked is not independently verified. Review, source-use eligibility and consumer delivery are separate. Record vendor statements, independent measurements and estimates distinctly.

Category comparison fields:

- Generated artifact ownership and export
- Backend, data and authentication scope
- Deployment and hosting options
- Editing, collaboration and versioning
- Usage limits, credits and recurring charges
