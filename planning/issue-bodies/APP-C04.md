# Translation and localization

## User decision

Applications that translate, localize or adapt text, speech and media for specific languages and audiences.

**State:** planned research. This issue does not establish verified product capabilities, publication rights, completed research or a delivered website.

## Scope

### Included

- Include an identifiable software product, independently discoverable product module, or software-delivered service that offers an AI-assisted end-user workflow within this definition; record the evidence for AI involvement.

### Excluded / existing owners

- Translation models and speech synthesis engines stay in T1.
- A listed language is not proof of quality for every direction, dialect or domain.

## Deliverables

- `research/categories/translation-localization.md`
- `research/candidates/translation-localization.json`
- `coverage/ledger.json`

## Research questions

- Which distinct products or independently identifiable modules document a material AI-assisted translation and localization workflow, and which discovered entities fail the inclusion rule?
- Which offerings, versions, languages, regions and access conditions qualify the evidence for language and direction coverage, terminology and glossary controls, media synchronization workflows?
- What user choices are changed by human review and quality evidence, data handling, output rights and geography, and what remains Unknown or disputed?
- Which claims are primary documentation, vendor claims, independent measurements or estimates; what separate source-use and receiving-owner decisions are still missing?

## Evidence and comparison rules

Use dated primary sources and the application contract. Bind claims to the exact product, release/channel, plan, platform, region and other applicable conditions. Preserve Unknown; source checked is not independently verified. Review, source-use eligibility and consumer delivery are separate. Record vendor statements, independent measurements and estimates distinctly.

Category comparison fields:

- Language and direction coverage
- Terminology and glossary controls
- Media synchronization workflows
- Human review and quality evidence
- Data handling, output rights and geography

Research territories (not verified capabilities):

- APP-C04.01: Document translation

- APP-C04.03: Speech interpretation

- APP-C04.05: Terminology and translation memory
- APP-C04.06: Low-resource and regional language tools
