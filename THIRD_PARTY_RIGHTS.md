# Third-party source and publication rights

The repository MIT license covers original contributions that contributors have authority to license. It does not grant rights to third-party descriptions, logos, screenshots, testimonials, reviews, databases, source compilations, benchmark outputs or linked software. Links do not transfer ownership or imply endorsement.

Reuse requires a recorded decision for the specific source, field, artifact/version and intended use. Treat research, automated retrieval, public display, caching, redistribution, download/export and API delivery as distinct uses. Record attribution and other conditions. Unknown or withdrawn permission blocks the affected publication projection; it does not require fabricating a negative capability finding.

Apply the canonical research [source policy](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/blob/main/research/T0-configuration/catalog-data-sources.md) and [publication handoff](https://github.com/Pick-Your-AI/pick-your-ai-research/blob/main/docs/PUBLICATION_ELIGIBILITY_HANDOFF.md). Application-specific decisions are proposals until accepted by the responsible reviewer. No blanket permission is created by this file.

Public GitHub commits are themselves publication and can enable bulk download. Keeping material out of a future website export does not authorize placing restricted or unapproved copied material in research notes, examples or other public repository files. Use original summaries and links under the research policy; raw source material requires its own permitted-use basis.
