# Application research taxonomy

Version 0.1.0 is an authored research scope proposal. Its categories and facets are not product findings or a claim of worldwide exhaustive coverage.

8 domains, 48 categories, 288 subcategories and 15 cross-cutting facets.

See [machine-readable taxonomy](taxonomy.json), [coverage ledger](../coverage/ledger.json) and [delivery backlog](../planning/BACKLOG.md).

## Classification rules

- Choose one main user workflow per product or independently identifiable module; additional supported workflows receive secondary category tags.
- Category membership is many-to-many; overlapping counts must not be added as distinct product totals.
- Subcategories are research territories, not claims that any named product covers them.
- Facet values describe one evidenced offering or claim scope; unknown remains unknown.
- Require evidence of a material AI-assisted workflow; vendor use of the word AI is a discovery signal only.
- Add or split a category only for a distinct user decision, evidence method or independently maintainable workflow; record taxonomy version and migration.

## Exclusions

- Foundation models, model releases, provider inference routes, accelerators, inference engines and generic infrastructure are owned by existing T0–T6 research; cross-link rather than clone.
- Pure libraries, SDKs, frameworks, protocols and connectors are excluded unless a distinct usable application product or application module can be identified.
- AI consultancy and implementation agencies without an identifiable software-delivered workflow remain service-provider notes, outside application counts.
- Directories, papers, datasets and benchmark results are sources or linked entities; their existence does not make them end-user applications.
- Announcements, demos, abandoned products and unconfirmed AI features remain labeled candidate notes; they do not automatically become active product records.
- No automatic copying of marketplace listings, logos, screenshots, reviews, terms or source databases without an eligible source-use decision.
